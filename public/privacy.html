Theophany Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Theophany processes information needed to provide its software-building and connected-service workflows.

Information we process

This may include session information, integration authorization data, and content needed to perform actions you request.

Connected services

When you connect a third-party service, Theophany stores authorization tokens securely on the server and uses them only to provide the connected functionality you request.

Security

OAuth tokens are encrypted before being stored in Theophany's server-side integration storage. Client secrets are kept server-side.

Your choices

You can stop using an integration and disconnect it when the corresponding Theophany control is available.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information provided by the Theophany service.
